## ✨ Overview
*My Apothecary Diaries* is a **historical mystery series** set in the imperial court of ancient China.  
Follow **Maomao**, a clever apothecary, as she solves mysterious cases with her sharp mind, knowledge of medicine, and no-nonsense attitude.  

> “Observation is everything. The truth is hiding in the smallest details.” – Maomao 

---

## 🎯 Why I Love It
- 🏺 Fascinating historical setting with beautiful imperial aesthetics  
- 🧪 Intriguing mix of **mystery, medicine, and court politics**  
- 👩‍⚕️ Strong, intelligent female protagonist who never gives up  
- 🔍 Every episode/chapter feels like a **puzzle waiting to be solved**
